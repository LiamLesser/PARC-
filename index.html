About PARC
Philadelphia Amateur Radio Club — serving the Delaware Valley since 1947.
Our Mission

The Philadelphia Amateur Radio Club (PARC), operating under callsign W3PHL, promotes the art and science of amateur radio communication and experimentation. We foster emergency preparedness, technical education, and camaraderie among licensed radio amateurs in the greater Philadelphia metropolitan area.


PARC is affiliated with the ARRL and serves as the primary ARES group for Philadelphia County under the Philadelphia Office of Emergency Management.
